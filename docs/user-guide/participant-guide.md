# Participant Guide

This guide is for people going on a trip. The first part walks through the steps: your profile and documents, applying, following your next steps, paying your trip costs, and your flights. The [Screen reference](#screen-reference) then describes every screen you use, field by field.

## The big picture

1. **Complete your profile.** Your profile holds your personal, contact, emergency contact, church, and health information. It's shared by every trip you apply for.
2. **Apply for a trip.** Choose your trip role and answer the application questions.
3. **Submit.** When your profile and application are complete, submitting makes your application **Pre-Approved**. That holds your place on the team.
4. **Upload your documents.** Add the documents your trip role and the trip need (for example a nursing license or a passport) to your profile. They're due 90 days before the trip starts.
5. **Get approved.** Trip staff review your documents and approve your application.
6. **Pay and prepare.** Make your deposit, raise or donate the rest, and enter your flights if you're booking them yourself.

Home's **My Trips** list always shows your next step for each trip.

## Your profile

Open **Profile** from the menu bar (or **Edit profile** in the user menu). Every field is described in [Edit profile](#edit-profile).

### What a complete profile needs

You can't submit an application until your profile is complete. A complete profile has:

- First name, middle name, last name, and email
- Address line 1, city, state/province, postal code, and country
- Phone country code and phone number
- Birthdate and gender
- Emergency contact name, country code, phone number, and relationship
- Current church home, with its city and state/province
- An allergies description, if you answer Yes to **Have allergies?**
- At least one medical condition from your organization's list, if you answer Yes to **Take medication?**
- At least one complete language row (language, fluency, and medical terminology), if you answer Yes to **Do you speak a language other than English?**

**Preferred name**, **Address line 2**, **Bio**, and the profile photo are optional.

While anything is missing, the top of the profile shows a **Missing:** list. It shrinks as you fill things in and disappears when the profile is complete.

### Once you're on a trip

While you have a Pre-Approved or Approved application on a trip that hasn't ended, your profile must stay complete. You can still change anything, but a save that would leave a required item empty is refused with "This profile is on an active trip and can't be saved with missing information." and a list of what's missing. Fill those in and save again. The same applies when staff edit your profile.

## Your documents

Documents live on your profile, in the **Documents** section, so one passport or license works for every trip.

### Adding a document

1. In your profile, choose **Add Document**.
2. Pick the **Document type**. Some types show instructions at the top of the dialog.
3. Fill in **Country issued**, **Issue date**, **Expiration date** (if the type has one), and **Document number** (if the type asks for one).
4. Attach the file: a photo or PDF. iPhone photos (HEIC) are converted automatically.
5. If the type has specializations (for example a board certification in Pediatrics), choose **Add specialization** for each one and give it a date and its own file.
6. Choose **Save**.

Files are stored encrypted. Only you and the trip staff who manage you can view them.

### What your trips need

When you have an application on an upcoming trip, the **Documents** section lists each trip with its due date (90 days before it starts) and the documents it needs. Each is marked **Uploaded** or **Missing**, and a missing one explains what to fix, for example "add an expiration date after 03/10/2027" or "select the Pediatrics specialization".

A trip can need:

- the documents your **trip role** requires (for example a Nurse needs a nursing license), including any required specializations;
- a **Passport**, if the trip requires one;
- a **Doctor Permission** document, if you're pregnant and the organization requires one.

### Expiration dates

A document has to be good for the trip, not just for today. Depending on its type, it must expire after the trip ends, on or after the trip ends, or 90 or 180 days after the trip ends. A passport that's valid today can still be too close to expiring for a trip next year. The trip's document list tells you when that happens.

In the document table, a red **Expired** tag shows on any document from its expiration date on, including the day it expires. Expired documents stay on your profile, and keep the tag after you add a newer one of the same type, so when you apply for a later trip you can see what needs replacing.

If you have several documents of the same type (say, an old and a new passport), each trip uses the one that expires last.

### Reviewed documents

Trip staff check each document and mark it **Reviewed**. You'll see a read-only **Reviewed** checkbox in your document list. A reviewed document can't be edited, replaced, or deleted, because staff have already checked it. If it needs to change, add a new document, or ask your trip staff.

If staff ask you to upload a document again, you'll get an email, and the document shows "Reload requested" with the date until you upload a new file.

## Applying for a trip

### Finding a trip

Home's **Trips you can apply for** lists open trips with how many positions are available. You can also start from your organization's public trip page and choose **Apply**.

- **Apply** shows when the trip has open spots.
- **Apply for Reserved** shows when staff reserved a spot for you by email and the open spots are gone.
- If there's no spot for you, you'll see "Thanks for your interest, but there are no spots available on this trip." or "Thanks for your interest, but all remaining spots on this trip are reserved."

### The application

The application asks for your trip role, funding (with an optional **Why go** story for your fundraising page), roommate preference, flight purchase choice, trip options, pregnancy (female applicants), and agreements. Every field is described in [Apply for trip / Update application](#apply-for-trip--update-application).

Your health and language answers come from your profile and show on the application as text. Change them in your profile, not on the application.

### Saving and submitting

- **Save Incomplete Application** saves your answers while something is still missing. The application stays **Incomplete**. It does not hold a role spot. A warning above the button says "Saving an Incomplete application does not secure your role on the trip. Complete the application and required profile fields to be able to submit the application which will secure the role." Clicking the button asks you to confirm with **OK**: "Your application data will be saved, but a role on the trip has not yet been secured." **OK** saves and closes the application. On the application page, **OK** saves and returns to Home.
- **Submit Application** appears when your profile and application are complete. A notice above the button says "Submitting the application will hold your role on the trip pending review of your documents once they are uploaded." Submitting makes it **Pre-Approved** and holds a role spot.
- **Update Application** saves changes to a Pre-Approved application. While the trip hasn't ended, it stays disabled until everything required is filled in, and a list shows what's missing.

Documents never block submitting. They're needed before approval.

When you reopen a saved application, a list shows anything still needed to submit. If your profile is incomplete, a warning names the missing fields and offers **Update profile**.

### After approval

Once your application is **Approved**, you can't edit it yourself, except **Why go**: change the story and choose **Save Why go** to update your fundraising page. If something else on it needs to change, contact your trip staff. They can move it back to Pre-Approved so you can edit it, then approve it again. You can still update your profile, as long as required fields stay filled in.

### Cancelling

Use **Cancel App** (from Home or the application) and confirm. A cancelled application stays on your list with **Uncancel**. Uncancelling brings it back as Incomplete or Pre-Approved, never straight to Approved, and only if your role still has a spot (or it was reserved for you).

## Your next steps

Home's **My Trips** shows each upcoming trip with your next step, color-coded: yellow when you need to act, orange while you're waiting on staff, and green when you're all set. The full list of messages and their buttons is in [Next steps](#next-steps).

## Paying for your trip

- Your **total trip cost** is the trip cost (adjusted by your trip options), plus your flight if the organization buys it, plus any add-on expenses staff add.
- Your **balance** is the total trip cost minus donations, never below $0.
- The **deposit** is the first amount the organization asks you to raise.
- On **My Trip**, **Make Donation** lets you pay toward your own balance. **View donations** lists what's been given.
- On Home, **Donate** in your next steps lets you give to yourself or a teammate.
- Friends and family can give through your fundraising page without an account.

Online donations are paid through Stripe, by credit card or bank account (ACH). **Pay with Stripe** takes you to Stripe's secure payment page; after paying you come back to a thank-you page. A card payment counts toward your balance right away. A bank payment shows as **Processing** for up to 4 business days and counts only once it **Succeeded**; a **Failed** payment never counts. If your organization hasn't connected Stripe, donation screens show "Online donations aren't set up for this organization yet." and you can't pay online.

## Your flights

- If the organization books your flight, staff enter it. You'll see it under **Flights** on **My Trip**.
- If you book your own, use **Add Flights** or **Edit Flights** on **My Trip** (or **Enter Flights** in your next steps) to enter each arrival and return segment. See [Flight segments](#flight-segments).
- Your planned arrival and departure dates are the trip dates, moved by any trip options with days before or after the trip. If your flights don't match, your next steps tell you.

## Emails you'll get

The organization can email you when your application is started, submitted, or approved; when a donation comes in, your deposit is reached, or you're fully paid; when staff ask you to upload a document again; and with reminders (incomplete application, missing documents, deposit or payment due, before the trip, trip arrival information). Trip staff can also email the team. Emails come from "*sender* via Mission Trips", and replies go to the organization or trip leader.

## Flight segments

Opened from **Add Flights**, **Edit Flights**, or **Enter Flights** when you book your own flights. Staff use the same dialog.

The top shows **Booked by**, your **Planned arrival** and **Planned departure** dates, and your **Flight notes**.

- **Confirmation number** — your booking reference (up to 50 characters).
- **Arrival Flight** — the flights that take you to the trip. **Add Segment** adds one leg.
- **Return Flight** — the flights home. **Add Segment** adds one leg.

Each segment ("Arrival segment 1", and so on) has:

| Field | Choices / notes |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Segment #** | The leg's order, starting at 1. Numbers can't repeat within Arrival or Return. |
| **Departure airport** | Every airport ("CODE — City"); type to search. A new leg starts with the previous leg's arrival airport. |
| **Arrival airport** | Same list. |
| **Airline** | Every airline ("CODE — Name"); type to search. |
| **Flight #** | The flight number. |
| **Class of travel** | Basic Economy, Economy, Premium Economy, Business, First (optional) |
| **Seat #** | Optional. |
| **Departure date** / **Departure time** | When the leg leaves. The first arrival leg starts on your planned arrival date; the first return leg on your planned departure date. |
| **Arrival date** / **Arrival time** | When the leg lands. It can't be before the departure. |

Airports, airline, flight number, and both dates and times are required for each leg. **Delete** removes a leg. **Save** saves everything; **Close** closes without saving.

### Donation return page

Where Stripe sends you after you pay. It shows one of these:

| You see | Means |
|---------|-------|
| "Thank you for your donation!", the amount, trip, and person, and **Receipt** | Your payment went through. **Receipt** opens Stripe's receipt in a new tab. |

| "Thank you for also covering the *fee* processing fee." (with either message above) | You added Stripe's processing fee. The amount line shows your gift; the fee is recorded separately. |

| "Waiting for Stripe to confirm your payment…" | The page checks with Stripe every 5 seconds, up to 6 times. |

| "This donation link has expired." | The payment page wasn't used within a day. |
| "Donation not found." | The link is wrong. |
